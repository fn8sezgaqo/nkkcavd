All of our content follows the following principles that we’ve created to guide our content creation.

Reliability: We understand that the information we provide is important for the health and well-being of our readers, and we take that responsibility very seriously. We use sources where we can and scan the internet for peoples reviews and opinions on compounds and brands.

Safety: will only discuss dosages based on what we find in the scientific literature. In addition, we are committed to legal compliance, ensuring that all our content is in compliance with local, state and federal laws, including but not limited to the FDA regulations for dietary supplements and other relevant regulations.

Honesty: We believe that providing accurate and honest information is crucial for the health and well-being of our readers. In terms of product reviews and evaluations, we are committed to providing transparent and honest opinions. We disclose any potential biases or conflicts of interest that may affect our reviews. We will only publish original content, and will not plagiarize from other sources.

Relevancy: We constantly update our informational content, so that our readers can be informed with the most up-to-date information and trends. We will be responsive to our readers’ feedback and will make changes to our content and editorial principles as necessary.

Brand Review Guidelines

Objectivity: We strive to be objective in our reviews and evaluations of brands. We do not accept money for specific outcomes (I.e., we don’t guarantee “good” reviews)

Research: We thoroughly research and fact-check all information provided in the brand reviews.

Product Testing: We use rigorous product testing methods for peptides, SARMs, and other supplements, to ensure that our reviews and evaluations are accurate and objective.

Product deliverability: If we recommend a brand, we order the products and test their QA to ensure its tested and delivered correctly.
