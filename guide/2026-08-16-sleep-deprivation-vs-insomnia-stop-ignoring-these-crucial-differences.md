Sleep deprivation and insomnia are related but distinct conditions. Sleep deprivation occurs when you don't get enough sleep, often due to lifestyle choices, work demands, or limited opportunity for rest. Insomnia, on the other hand, is a sleep disorder where you struggle to fall asleep or stay asleep even when you have the time and ideal conditions to do so.

Understanding which one you're experiencing is the first step toward better rest and better health. Because symptoms can overlap with other conditions—like anxiety, thyroid issues, or sleep apnea—it's worth getting clarity before trying random fixes.
