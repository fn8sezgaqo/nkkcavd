⚡️ Cellular Rejuvenation or Hype? Do NAD+ Supplements Work in 2025

Photo by Alora Griffiths on Unsplash

NAD+ has become a buzzword in anti-aging and metabolic health circles, but do oral NAD+ boosters live up to the hype? This 2025 guide breaks down the science behind supplementation and what it really means for energy, brain health, and longevity.

What’s Covered:

⚡️ Mechanisms of ActionNAD+ (nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide) is a vital coenzyme involved in cellular energy production, DNA repair, and metabolic signalling. Supplement forms like NR (nicotinamide riboside) and NMN (nicotinamide mononucleotide) aim to increase intracellular NAD+ levels to counteract age-related decline.

⚡️ Best Use Cases & TimingNAD+ boosters are best taken consistently over time. Stacking with resveratrol, TMG, or CoQ10 may enhance effectiveness. Liposomal and sublingual forms show promise for bypassing digestive breakdown.

🧬 Decoding Muscle Disorders: A Guide to Muscular Dystrophy Types

Photo by Rohit Reddy on Unsplash

Muscular dystrophy isn’t a single condition—it’s a group of genetic disorders that cause progressive muscle weakness. This guide outlines the most common types and how they affect the body, diagnosis, and long-term care.

🧬 Mechanisms of ActionEach form of muscular dystrophy involves mutations that disrupt muscle protein production—especially dystrophin. This leads to gradual muscle degeneration, weakness, and impaired function over time.

🧬 Types of Muscular DystrophyThe article breaks down major types, including Duchenne, Becker, Myotonic, Facioscapulohumeral (FSHD), and Limb-Girdle. It explains onset age, symptoms, genetic inheritance, and expected progression for each.

🧬 Support, Diagnosis & Emerging TherapiesWhile there’s no cure yet, early diagnosis enables supportive care like physical therapy, orthotics, gene therapy trials, and corticosteroids. Telehealth is helping improve access to care and monitoring in 2025.

💥 Mechanisms of Muscle GrowthSteroids increase muscle mass by enhancing nitrogen retention, protein synthesis, and recovery speed. Natural athletes rely on consistent progressive overload, optimal nutrition, and genetic potential.

💥 Advantages & DrawbacksEnhanced bodybuilding accelerates growth, size, and density—but comes with risks like hormonal shutdown, organ strain, and long-term health issues. Natural bodybuilding is safer and sustainable, though slower and often limited by plateaus.
