# Training vs. Exercise

If you are "working out" 6 days per week, I want you to change your mindset about exercise.

Listen, don't take this personally, but you just can't do high-intensity, body-changing workouts 6 days per week. The intensity of your workouts will suffer as the week goes on, and even week to week based on cumulative fatigue. Training 6 days per week just doesn't give your body time to recover, repair, and regenerate.

If you workout at a gym, or did in the past, you probably remember "that guy" who used to come in much less frequently than you, but somehow seemed to make more improvements than you. He got stronger faster, and built more muscle, and always seemed lean.

[Training vs. Exercise](https://peplawllc.com/blog/0918784111.html)

Of course, he was on drugs, right? Well yeah, if proper recovery was a drug.

[Training vs. Exercise](https://thymosin-report.hbsnzj.com/blog/0246634320.html)

If you are doing hard workouts everyday, you aren't recovering, you aren't improving your fitness or your body as fast as you want (or you can). You need to make a mindset change.

[Training vs. Exercise](https://skinsequence.escortskhi.com/blog/6193525722.html)

And that change is: Separate your workouts into three "training days" and four "exercise days".

Training days represent balls-hard workouts. These can be your hard runs or long runs (if you are a runner), or your total body weight training and interval workouts (if you want to lose fat and gain muscle), or your upper-lower weight training splits (if you just want to build muscle).

As for your exercise days, you should have 7 of these per week. Basically, get exercise everyday. But exercise just means getting off your butt and moving for 30 minutes. If you are a runner, your exercise days should be focused on recovery methods, light activity, and some mobility exercises (because chances are you are stiff as a corpse from the combination of running and being a desk jockey).

[Training vs. Exercise](https://regeneration-desk.hbsnzj.com/)

[Training vs. Exercise](https://thymosin-report.hbsnzj.com/blog/7865264858.html)

[Training vs. Exercise](https://lumina-dermis.sunsimiaosd.com/blog/9703831722.html)

If you want to lose fat, but you are a busy dad, spend your exercise time playing with the kids, walking the dogs, and doing work around the house. And take some of the time you saved from cutting back on your workouts to work on your nutrition - that is the key to fat loss . What good is spending 6 hours in the gym per week if it means you don't have time to buy the right foods and prepare them so that you don't eat from a vending machine or grab "something on the go"?

So no matter what your situation, you should only do 3 training days per week, and allow yourself to recover on your exercise days. You'll be amazed at how much better you feel in the gym on those 3 training days when you cut the volume back.
