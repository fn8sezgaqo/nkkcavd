The world's richest human, Jeff Bezos, somehow found time in his busy schedule to see Rampage, also known as Dwayne "The Rock" Johnson's giant gorilla movie. Because every film The Rock makes is genetically engineered to be enjoyable, the Amazon CEO offered his enthusiastic praise.

"I love Dwayne Johnson... Still working on my smolder," Bezos tweeted, a reference to Johnson's incredibly named character Smolder Bravestone in Jumanji: Welcome to the Jungle.

In typical fashion, The Rock responded.

"Haha brother the love and admiration is mutual. Mahalo to you and the kids for seeing RAMPAGE. Drinks and dinner on me one day soon please," he wrote. "And kindly walk into your next shareholder’s [meeting] with that bad ass smolder."

Just a couple of insanely wealthy buddies being nice on social media. Now, the question isn't whether or not Jeff Bezos should co-star in Rampage 2: Amazon Primate — because that's obviously a yes — but when these two are going to fit in a workout session already.
