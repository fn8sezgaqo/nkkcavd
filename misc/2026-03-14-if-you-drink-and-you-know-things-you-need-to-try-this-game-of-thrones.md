# If You Drink and You Know Things, You Need to Try This Game of Thrones Wine

Do you drink and know things? Then read on, friend!

Game of Thrones' eighth and final season is going to be one of the most epic spectacles in the history of TV. In the lead-up to the big event, we've seen a slew of GOT-themed products hit the market. There’s Game of Thrones beer from the Ommegang brewery in New York, where every brew resembles a character or theme—like Sour Blonde for Cersei. There are even Game of Thrones Oreos coming soon (although it sounds like they're just regular old Oreos in special packaging).

Here's another treat to crank your viewing parties up a notch: GOT-themed wine. Check out the new vintages of Game of Thrones wine, created by award-winning winemaker Bob Cabral. If you've already tried the collection and loved it, you'll be happy to know there are more varieties to try this season. If you haven't, give them a shot and celebrate the show’s final season—which begins on April 14—the right way.

The following three wines can be purchased online, for $20 each: Game of Thrones 2016 Chardonnay, Central Coast, CA; Game of Thrones 2017 Pinot Noir, Willamette Valley; and Game of Thrones 2017 Red Blend, Paso Robles, CA. Tyrion Lannister would approve.

Each purchase comes with a free GOT-themed corkscrew, and every bottle contains a cork that’s imprinted with either the House Tyrell or House Martell sigil. Definitely worth saving, right?

Grab a bottle, turn on HBO, and toast to the end of a great show. Of course, you might want to keep it on-hand for when at least half of your favorite characters (presumably) kick the bucket.
