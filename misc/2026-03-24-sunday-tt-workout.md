# Sunday TT Workout

[Sunday TT Workout](https://metabolic-horizon.bbsxyxy.com/blog/0774117847.html)

Workouts are going great, energy levels are off the charts, and strength in a lot of my exercises is as good as ever...so I was jumping at the chance to do today's workout. I was going to do it late afternoon, but couldn't hold off.

[Sunday TT Workout](https://neurocurrent.china-akan.com/)

I went in, and before I lifted, I wandered into the basketball court and tried to touch the rim. And I can still do it, not bad for a 33-year old, 5'8" white guy.

After I went straight to deadlifts, and hit a new personal best at 375 overhand grip for 1 rep, no straps. And to my credit, the bar was smooth, and no knurling for grip.

Strength

Overhand grip, 375x1

Alternate grip, 355x5, 355x3 (didn't do third set)
