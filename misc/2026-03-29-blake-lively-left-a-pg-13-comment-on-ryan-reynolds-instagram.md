# Blake Lively Left A PG-13 Comment on Ryan Reynolds’ Instagram

[PG- Comment on](https://musclemend.sunsimiaosd.com/blog/0656105279.html)

Ryan Reynolds is most famous, these days, for his role in Deadpool—a foul-mouthed mercenary with neither a filter nor much of a face. His next film, though, comes to theaters May 10, where he'll voice the titular Detective Pikachu, in what just might be the most outlandish, sensational piece of cinema of the year (or maybe I'm just a 90's kid with a soft spot for Pokémon).

But nonetheless, Reynolds is already starting his promotion for the genre-hybrid film, posting an Instagram hyping both his voice role as Pikachu, along with some of his Pokémon brethren. "Footage from my ACTUAL audition for #DetectivePikachu," he said in the caption.
