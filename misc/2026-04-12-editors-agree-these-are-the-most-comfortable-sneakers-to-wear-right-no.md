To find the most comfortable sneakers, we consulted a panel of three fitness editors and our fashion director who tested more than 50 kicks for our 2023 Men's Health Sneaker Awards. We also asked podiatrist Dr. Brad Schaeffer, D.P.M, on what to look for in the most comfortable sneakers. His advice? Look for insoles that are super supportive, especially in the heel or arch, as well as ample bounce and cushioning to limit the strain on your feet, legs, and knees. Sneakers made with breathable knits are great for workout trainers, or if you sweat a lot. For guys with wide feet, look for brands that make shoes in a variety of widths.

Cole Haan GrandPrø Crew Wingtip Sneakers

Walking the line between sneaker and oxford shoe like a tightrope artist, the GrandPrø Crew Wingtip is the most comfortable wintip shoe we've ever tried. The leather upper has a dress shoe toe, but a sneaker-style padded ankle. Despite having a chunky sole, it is exceptionally lightweight thanks to a proprietary FlowerFlow EVA material made with 24% dandelion.

Our style editors like this as an office shoe because it's formal enough to meet most dress codes while feeling like some of our favorite walking shoes. The wingtip leather upper and thick sole looks great with dress pants or a full suit. Want something a little more formal? Go with the black-on-black color way.

Koio Capri

Handmade in Italy from premium leather and recycled rubber, Koio uses a level of craftsmanship typically reserved for dress shoes. The Capri is one of the most stylish leather sneakers we've tested because it is sleek and simple. Better still, it is exceptionally comfortable thanks to an OrthoLite® Hybrid™ insole.

All white sneakers work well with formalwear, but we're here to tell you that white sneakers with gum soles work even better. The contrast provides a look closer to a dress shoe while still being crisp and clean. The Capri is probably the best example out there because of how comfortable it is.

SeaVees Baja Slip On Original

Where most sneakers achieve comfort by building layers of support and cushioning, SeaVees gets the job done with a minimalist design. The linen canvas upper is exceptionally light and breathable, practically molding to your foot like a sock. While the a memory foam footbed provides a little bounce with every step. You also get surprisingly good grip from the vulcanized rubber outsole.

Our style editors would single out the Baja Slip On as an essential summer shoe because of the simple, breezy, and classic design. Yet, it works perfectly well year round in most climates. This is just the easiest shoe to wear with comfort that far exceeds expectations.

Salomon X Ultra 4

Stafford describes the X Ultra 4 as, "lightweight, agile, and stable meet tough, technical, and tacky (as in "grippy")." That's everything you want in a good hiking shoe. Its chassis provides optimal support without limiting range of movement.

When it comes to hiking shoes, comfort is as much about keeping the elements out as it is about whats inside. Salomon uses a Gore-Tex lining to keep moisture out, which is just as useful for slick city streets or rain-soaked trails.

Nike Invincible 3

Nike rates the support as High and the cushioning as Super High for the Invincible 3, and you can feel it in every step. "The key is the foam, which is super bouncy and responsive—and the makeover from the first two iterations of the model actually make it look good off the road, too," Senior Editor Brett Williams said in his testing.

MBT Sport 1

Orthopedic shoes don't exactly have the most stylish reputation, but MBT is working to change that. The self proclaimed "physiological footwear" brand can stand toe-to-toe with many of the biggest athletic brands in looks. When it comes to comfort, the Sport 1 is an absolute stand out.

Podiatrist Dr. Brad Schaeffer highly recommends MBT. "I personally wear these when walking around, standing, work outs," he said. "They do it all, in my opinion." The brand is making some of the most comfortable every-day kicks right now, and should be on your radar.

Asics Jogger X81

Asics does retro running shoes with the best of them. In the case of the Jogger X81, you get a combination of throw back looks with modern comfort. The uppers are fully inspired by the Japanese brand's golden age of the late '70s and early '80s. Which is contrasted by an innovative outsole that provides exceptional spring and cushioning.

This is a flashy every-day sneaker that delivers ample comfort. We wouldn't run a 5K in them, but they make great casual sneakers. There is a reassuring heft to them that you don't get with lightweight trainers.
