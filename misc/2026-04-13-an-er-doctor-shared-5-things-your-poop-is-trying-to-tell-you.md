Everybody poops; it's a fact of life. A lesser-known fact, though, is that your poop can actually be an indicator of broader health concerns. In a new video, E.R. doctor and YouTuber Jordan Wagner lists five instances in which your bowel movements are also red flags.

Pencil-thin poop

"Stool that is occasionally very thin is not a cause for concern; it's likely due to muscle contractions in the large intestine," says Wagner. "But if there's a sudden or consistent change in your stool that's always thin, it could mean there's a blockage in your colon, and you should see you doctor."
