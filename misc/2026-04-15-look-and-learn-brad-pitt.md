# Look and Learn: Brad Pitt

Brad Pitt knows how to take a risk—and we’re not just talking about dating a woman who used to wear a vial of her boyfriend’s blood around her neck. We’re referring to his facial hair. From clean-shaven to straggly-hobo beard, Pitt’s grooming choices are more diverse than his family. Here’s what you can learn from his most recent look

1. He has some expression lines, showing that he didn’t fall prey to the Botox trend. Yes, he’s aging, but he’s doing it gracefully.

2. Even with constant travel and a growing brood at home, he still remembers to take care of his skin. It’s not oily, but not dry, giving him a youthful glow. Try Sisleyum for Men Anti-Age Global Revitalizer to moisturize and reduce signs of aging. ($265, bloomingdales.com)

3. Guys have one big advantage over women: gray hair is a positive sign of maturity. Still, that doesn’t mean you should go full-on Benjamin Button. Keep it short—like Pitt’s—to embrace your grays without flaunting them.

4. The high collar frames his face nicely and it’s a nice contrast to his rugged, unshaven look.
