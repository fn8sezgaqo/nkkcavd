As if going to the dentist isn't torturous enough, Travis Barker had to go and up the ante by several levels, getting a full-blown knee tattoo during his latest trip to the dentist chair.

In an Instagram video posted on January 29, Barker documented the completely normal task of getting inked at the dentist. Though no one is working on his mouth during the six-second clip, you can clearly see dental instruments in the background while his tattoo artist goes in on his ink.

[Watch Travis Barker](https://aegispeptide.snaketongueofficial.com/blog/8308109615.html)

"Multitasking at the dentist," Barker captioned the clip, adding a tooth emoji and a blood emoji. Forget hybrid Taco Bell and Pizza Hut locations—hybrid tattoo studios and dentist offices deserve all the hype.

[Barker Get Tattoo](https://tendonforge.dns0663.com/)

Barker's tattoo artist appeared to be adding thorns to his long-standing skull tattoo that covers the drummer's left knee cap. It's a particularly sensitive spot due to the prominent bone that likely made for the worst cleaning ever.

[of Dentist Appointment](https://reboundworks.escortskhi.com/)

Shockingly, Barker isn't the first celebrity to get inked at the dentist. No, apparently this is a trend. Back in 2020, Post Malone got tattooed at the dentist, telling Jimmy Kimmel that he wanted to kill "two birds with one stone."

The heavily-tattooed rapper said he approached his tattoo artist with the unusual suggestion, figuring he'd be sitting around for hours anyway working on his smile.

"Why not get two bad things out of the way at the same time?" Post Malone told Kimmel.

Lesson: Next time you go to the dentist, just bring along your personal tattoo artist. They'll be totally cool with it, right?
