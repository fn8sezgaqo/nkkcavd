# Win Her Over

When AXE approached New Girl’s Max Greenfield with their “Fear No Susan Glenn” campaign, he was right on board. “It’s not just about talking to women, it’s about not letting fear get in the way of what you want and not having any ‘what ifs’ later in life,” says Greenfield. Here, we catch up with prime time’s favorite funny guy to find out how you can win over the woman of your dreams.

Who is Susan Glenn and what does she represent? She’s the girl who you’ve always wanted to talk to, but you’re afraid to even say “hello.” I thought it was such a great concept so I wrote and directed this web series based on the idea (see the first episode above). We followed one guy’s journey as he tries to talk to his “Susan Glenn.”

How would you describe your style? Simple. My character wears a lot of V-neck tees, but I’m not a V-neck kind of guy. I like a crew neck. Splendid has the most amazing tees. They’re the most comfortable shirts on the planet. It’s like wearing appropriate pajamas. I don’t care how nice the clothes are; if I’m not comfortable in them, I won’t wear them. Even if you’re going on a date, keep it simple. I’m a big fan of dark jeans and simple boots.

You’re married with a 2-year-old daughter. How has your style evolved since you met your wife? She doesn’t like any kind of sweats or “athletic clothes,” as she calls them. My style has gotten a lot better since I’ve been married. I used to look like a coach everyday. I would even wear sweatshirts on dates.
