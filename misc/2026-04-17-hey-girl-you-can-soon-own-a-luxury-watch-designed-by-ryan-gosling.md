As if you needed another reason to covet TAG Heuer watches, along comes Ryan Gosling. The 40-year-old actor is the new face of the luxury Swiss watch brand, but the actor is more than just a handsome mug to sell timepieces retailing in the thousands. According to a recent WWD interview with TAG Heuer CEO Frédéric Arnault, Gosling will actually design his own luxury watch with the brand.

"His involvement goes beyond a typical ambassador," Arnault told WWD, adding, "He's going to design his own watch for himself. There are many things we're going to do together."
