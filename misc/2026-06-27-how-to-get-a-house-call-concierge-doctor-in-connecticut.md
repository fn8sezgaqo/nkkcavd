What are the advantages of concierge doctors over clinics?

Avoiding the administrative burdens of processing insurance claims allows concierge physicians to operate a more patient-focused practice. Concierge physicians typically have fewer patients overall and can offer longer appointment times than traditional practices.

Other benefits of concierge medicine include:

Schedule Flexibility: House call concierge doctors can administer appointments at your home or office, allowing you access to healthcare without disrupting your busy schedule.

Stronger Relationships: Many concierge practices offer additional support via telehealth services in addition to longer appointments, allowing you to give health updates, ask questions, and get advice for your general wellness needs.

Avoiding Covid-19 and other contagious illnesses: Skipping waiting rooms at physician’s offices greatly lessens the likelihood of being exposed to Covid-19 and other highly transmissible illnesses.
