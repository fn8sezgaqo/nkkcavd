What is Pleural Mesothelioma?

Pleural mesothelioma is a rare cancer that occurs in the thin tissue layer around the lungs. It's more common in people over 50 years old. The most likely cause is long-term exposure to a mineral called "asbestos".

Typical Symptoms of Pleural Mesothelioma

Exposure to dust, asbestos, or chemicals at work

Chest pain when coughing or during deep breathing

Dry cough

Difficulty breathing

Unintentional weight loss of more than 5% or 4.4lbs/2kg within one month

Fever

Cough

Back pain or discomfort

Diagnostic Questions for Pleural Mesothelioma

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this disease:

Have you been exposed to dust, asbestos, or chemicals at work?

Do you have sharp chest pain when coughing or taking a deep breath?

Do you have a dry cough?

Are you experiencing difficulty breathing?

Have you experienced unintentional weight loss of more than 5% or 4.4lbs/2kg within one month?

Treatment of Pleural Mesothelioma

Treatment for pleural mesothelioma typically includes surgery, radiation therapy, chemotherapy, immunotherapy, or a combination of these treatments.
