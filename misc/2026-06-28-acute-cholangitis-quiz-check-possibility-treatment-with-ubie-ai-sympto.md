What is Acute Cholangitis?

Inflammation of the bile ducts near the liver. It can be caused by stones or tumors blocking the liver's drainage system. This often results in a serious infection. Prompt medical treatment is crucial as it can be life-threatening.

Typical Symptoms of Acute Cholangitis

Stomach or abdominal pain that worsens after meals

History of bile duct infection or inflammation, i.e. cholangitis

Stomach or abdominal pain in a specific area

Stomach or abdominal pain that prevents deep breathing

Fever

Yellowish eyes or skin

Pain in the upper right abdomen

Having difficulty urinating or producing less urine

Diagnostic Questions for Acute Cholangitis

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this disease:

Do you have stomach or abdominal pain that worsens after meals?

Have you been diagnosed with gallbladder inflammation (cholangitis) or treated with a bile duct stent?

Do you have stomach or abdominal pain in a specific area?

Are you experiencing stomach or abdominal pain that prevents you from taking deep breaths?

Do you have a fever?

Treatment of Acute Cholangitis

Treatment may include antibiotics to kill the bacteria, and a procedure or surgery to remove the blockage. This may require hospitalization for a period of time.
