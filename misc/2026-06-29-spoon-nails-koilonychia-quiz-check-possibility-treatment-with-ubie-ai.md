What is Spoon Nails (Koilonychia)?

This condition causes nails to appear scooped out, resembling a spoon shape. It can be seen in iron deficiency, vitamin b deficiency, and sometimes other medication conditions like diabetes, heart disease and thyroid disorders.

Typical Symptoms of Spoon Nails (Koilonychia)

Change in nail shape

Change in nail color

Diagnostic Questions for Spoon Nails (Koilonychia)

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this disease:

Did your nail shape change?

Did your nail color change?

Treatment of Spoon Nails (Koilonychia)

Any underlying causes must be treated first. (e.g., with iron or vitamin B supplements). Meanwhile, maintaining short and clean nails, avoiding nail biting and filing the nail regularly can help.
