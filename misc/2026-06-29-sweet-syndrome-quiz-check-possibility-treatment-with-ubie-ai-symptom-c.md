What is Sweet Syndrome?

A rare skin condition with sudden fever and painful rash on arms, legs, trunk, face, or neck. Most Sweet syndrome cases develop after upper respiratory or gastrointestinal infections or in people with certain cancers. Sometimes, it can be triggered by specific medications.

Typical Symptoms of Sweet Syndrome

Ring-like rash

Fever

A lump or bump in the mouth

Pus-filled blisters or bumps on the skin

Skin problem on the chest, tummy or back

Multiple areas of skin redness or red spots

Swelling in the affected area

Blisters on the skin

Diagnostic Questions for Sweet Syndrome

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this disease:

Do you have swollen red rings or circles on your hands, legs, or body?

Do you have a fever?

Have you noticed a lump or bump in your mouth?

Do you have pus-filled blisters or bumps on your skin?

Are there any skin problems on your stomach, chest, or back?

Treatment of Sweet Syndrome

Sweet syndrome treatment involves medications to reduce inflammation. If an underlying cause is found, it should also be treated to prevent recurrence.
