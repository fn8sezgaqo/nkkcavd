What is Amebic Dysentery?

Infection of the gut with microorganisms called amoeba. It can be caused by contaminated food and water.

Typical Symptoms of Amebic Dysentery

Blood in stool

Fever

Attracted to others of the same sex

Pain in the lower left abdomen

Stomach or abdominal pain

Frequent stomach or abdominal pain with unknown causes

Diarrhea or loose stools

Nausea or vomiting

Diagnostic Questions for Amebic Dysentery

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this disease:

Have you noticed any blood in your stool?

Do you have a fever?

Do you identify as a homosexual?

Do you feel pain in your lower left abdomen?

Do you have any stomach or abdominal pain?

Treatment of Amebic Dysentery

Treatment involves antibiotics to kill the amoeba. This may take several weeks to ensure the parasites are fully cleared. In severe cases of dehydration, hospitalization may be necessary for monitoring and administering fluids through a needle in the vein.
