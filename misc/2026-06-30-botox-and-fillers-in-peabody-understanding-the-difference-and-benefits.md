What Are Dermal Fillers?

Dermal fillers are injectable treatments, most commonly made with hyaluronic acid, a substance naturally found in the body. Fillers are used to restore lost volume, enhance contours, and smooth static wrinkles.

Fillers provide immediate results, with longevity ranging from 6 to 18 months, depending on the product used and treatment area.
