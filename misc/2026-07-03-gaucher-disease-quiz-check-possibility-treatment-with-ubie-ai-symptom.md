What is Gaucher Disease?

GM1 gangliosidosis is a genetic disorder that progressively destroys nerve cells in the brain and spinal cord. Some researchers categorize this condition into three types based on the age when symptoms first appear.

Diagnostic Questions for Gaucher Disease

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this disease:

Have you experienced any seizures?

Was that your first seizure ever?

Did you have a seizure in the past week?

Do you feel a hard lump in your upper right abdomen?

Did you feel confused or disoriented after the seizure?

Treatment of Gaucher Disease

There is no cure for Gaucher disease. Treatment options for types 1 and 3 include medication and enzyme replacement therapy, which is typically very effective. There is no good treatment for the brain damage of types 2 and 3.
