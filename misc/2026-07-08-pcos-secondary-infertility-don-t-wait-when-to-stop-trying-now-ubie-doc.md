Couples with PCOS may consider stopping conception attempts under several circumstances: when fertility treatments have been unsuccessful over an extended period, when the emotional toll of ongoing efforts becomes overwhelming, or when the financial burden of continued treatment is no longer sustainable. This decision is deeply personal and often benefits from guidance by a fertility specialist, mental health professional, or support group.

Explanation

Deciding when to stop trying to conceive can be difficult for couples with PCOS. Based on the provided references, it is important to consider several factors.

Duration and Success of Treatment: If a couple has been undergoing treatment for a long time without success, they might consider stopping. This could mean multiple cycles of treatments like in vitro fertilization (IVF) without achieving pregnancy.
