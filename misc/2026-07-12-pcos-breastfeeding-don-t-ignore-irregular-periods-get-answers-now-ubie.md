# PCOS & Breastfeeding: Don’t Ignore Irregular Periods—Get Answers Now | Ubie Doctor's Note

Why might women with PCOS experience irregular periods while breastfeeding?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

[Ubie Doctor's Note](https://allpepay.com/)

Women with PCOS may experience irregular periods while breastfeeding due to the natural effects of breastfeeding on ovulation and fertility.

Breastfeeding can naturally delay the return of regular menstrual cycles. This is because breastfeeding affects the body's hormone levels, which can suppress ovulation.

How Breastfeeding Affects Menstrual Cycles:

Hormonal Influence: Breastfeeding increases levels of a hormone called prolactin, which can suppress ovulation.

[Irregular Periods Get Answers](https://satietybrief.china-akan.com/)

Ovulation Suppression: Without regular ovulation, periods can be irregular or absent.

Women with PCOS already have irregular ovulation patterns. When combined with the effects of breastfeeding, this can lead to even more irregular periods.

What This Means:

Delayed Periods: It may take longer for women with PCOS to see their periods return after giving birth.

Varied Experiences: Each woman's experience can differ, with some seeing a return to regular cycles sooner than others.

Breastfeeding is a natural process that affects all women differently, and those with PCOS may notice more pronounced changes in their menstrual cycles.
