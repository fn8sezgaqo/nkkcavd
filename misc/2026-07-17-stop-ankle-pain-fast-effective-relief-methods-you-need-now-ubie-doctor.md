Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Ankle pain relief typically involves a combination of approaches: topical anti-inflammatory patches such as diclofenac, rest and immobilization, and, in more severe cases, medications administered in emergency settings. The right treatment depends on the cause and severity of your symptoms, which can range from a minor sprain to a fracture or underlying condition requiring urgent care.
