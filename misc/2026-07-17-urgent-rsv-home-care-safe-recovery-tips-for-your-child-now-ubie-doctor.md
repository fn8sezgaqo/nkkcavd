Explanation

Respiratory Syncytial Virus (RSV) is a common virus that can affect children, especially infants. To help a child recover safely from RSV at home, there are several steps parents can take:

Rest: Make sure your child gets plenty of rest. This helps their body fight the virus.

Hydration: Keep your child well-hydrated by offering plenty of fluids. This helps with mucus production and keeps the throat moist.

Avoid Irritants: Keep your child away from smoke and other irritants that can make breathing harder.
