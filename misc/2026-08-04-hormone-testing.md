Hormones are chemicals that deliver messages throughout your body and help your body both develop and function normally. Hormones are produced by your endocrine system, a collection of organs including your thyroid, pancreas, adrenal glands, and pituitaries. Your hormones are in balance when your endocrine system is working normally. However, any single organ producing too many or too few hormones can throw the entire system off. Because your hormones affect so many different parts of your body, it doesn’t take a huge change in your body’s levels for you to feel a difference in your health or your mood.

Hormone testing can benefit anyone. Imbalances in your hormones can be caused by several different factors including diet, stress, lifestyle, genetics, toxins, or other environmental factors.

Identifying whether your hormones are out of balance is essential to help you know when to take corrective action. Depending on the exact hormone (or hormones) out of balance, you may need to make changes in your diet, fitness, supplements, lifestyle adjustments, or hormone therapy. We also test for the levels of cortisol – your main stress hormone – to help identify whether you may benefit from stress management techniques or other stress-reducing lifestyle changes.
