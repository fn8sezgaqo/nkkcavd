How is obesity diagnosed using BMI?

Obesity is diagnosed using Body Mass Index (BMI), a calculation based on a person's weight and height. A BMI of 30 or higher indicates obesity, while 25–29.9 is considered overweight and 18.5–24.9 is a healthy range. Doctors may also assess waist circumference, body fat percentage, and related health conditions like high blood pressure or diabetes for a more complete diagnosis.
