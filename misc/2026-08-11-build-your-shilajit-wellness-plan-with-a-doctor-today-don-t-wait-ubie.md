1. Understand What Shilajit Is

Shilajit forms over centuries as plant materials decompose between layers of rock. It's often processed into a resin, powder, or capsule. Key components include:

Fulvic AcidSupports nutrient transport and antioxidant defenses.

MineralsOver 80 trace minerals—iron, magnesium, zinc—important for energy and cellular health.

AntioxidantsHelp neutralize free radicals and reduce inflammation.

Why It Matters for Total Body WellnessCombining shilajit's nutrient profile with a healthy diet, exercise, and stress management can support energy, immunity, cognitive function, and overall vitality.

5. Determine the Right Dosage and Schedule

General guidelines can vary, so always follow product instructions and your doctor's advice. Typical starting doses:

Resin Form: 150–250 mg per day (about pea-sized)

Powder/Capsules: 300–500 mg per day

Best practices:

Start Low: Begin with half the recommended dose for one week to assess tolerance.

Consistency: Take shilajit at the same time each day, preferably with food to improve absorption.

Adjust Gradually: If well tolerated, increase to the full dose over 2–4 weeks.

6. Integrate Shilajit into Your Daily Routine

Shilajit is only one piece of a total body wellness plan. Combine it with:

Balanced NutritionEat whole foods rich in fruits, vegetables, lean proteins, and healthy fats.

Regular ExerciseAim for at least 150 minutes of moderate activity per week.

Stress ManagementPractice mindfulness, yoga, or deep-breathing exercises.

Adequate SleepTarget 7–9 hours per night for optimal recovery.

HydrationDrink plenty of water to support nutrient transport and detoxification.

By layering these habits, shilajit can complement—not replace—your healthy lifestyle.
