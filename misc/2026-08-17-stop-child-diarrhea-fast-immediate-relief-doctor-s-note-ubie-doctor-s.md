Explanation

Diarrhea in children can lead to dehydration, so it is important to act quickly. Oral Rehydration Solutions (ORS): These are special drinks made with sugar and salt that help replace lost fluids. According to the research by Lukmanji, these solutions are effective in managing dehydration caused by diarrhea. You can find ORS packets at most pharmacies.

[Stop Child Diarrhea Fast](https://incretin-path.escortskhi.com/blog/8884315918.html)

[Fast Immediate](https://cortexcompass.jcesqc8.com/blog/0943800741.html)

Intravenous Rehydration: If a child is severely dehydrated and unable to drink ORS, healthcare providers may use intravenous fluids. Research by Bender and Ozuah discusses the use of intravenous rehydration in cases of gastroenteritis. This method quickly restores fluid balance in the body.

Fluids to Avoid: Avoid giving sugary drinks or sodas as they can worsen diarrhea.

When to Seek Medical Help: If the child shows signs of severe dehydration, such as a very dry mouth, no tears when crying, or decreased urination, see a doctor immediately. You can also use this free symptom checker to help assess your child's symptoms and determine the appropriate level of care needed. Medical professionals can provide the necessary care, which may include intravenous fluids as per the study by Mahajan and colleagues.
