Why Internal Line Cleaning Matters

Gas Flow & Burner PerformanceDebris, spider webs or rodent nests can block orifices and burner ports, causing uneven flames, flare-ups or pilot outage.

Fire & Explosion RiskA partially clogged line can cause gas to accumulate, potentially leading to flash fires or small explosions when you ignite the grill.

Food Safety & TasteOld grease, nesting material or droppings can contaminate drippings and smoke, affecting flavor and potentially exposing you to bacteria.

Equipment LongevityRoutine cleaning prevents rust and corrosion inside the grill housing, hoses and burner tubes, extending the life of your investment.

Recognizing a Mouse Nest in Your Grill

Musty or ammonia-like odor when opening the lid

Visible debris, twigs, shredded paper or insulation around burners

Droppings near control knobs, grease tray or propane tank area

Evidence of chewing on hoses or seals

If you spot any of these signs, remove the propane tank immediately and follow the cleaning steps below before proceeding.

Step-by-Step: Cleaning Internal Lines & Components

Shut Off & Disconnect

Turn off burners and close the propane tank valve.

Disconnect the tank from the regulator.

Move your grill to a well-ventilated outdoor area, away from structures.

Disassemble Grill Interior

Remove grates, flavorizer bars, heat shields and burner tubes.

Lay these parts on a clean, flat surface for separate cleaning.

Clear Debris & Vacuum

Use the shop vacuum with a narrow nozzle to extract loose debris, nest fragments and droppings from inside the grill firebox.

Pay special attention around the burner inlets and control valve area.

[Doctor's Warning Clean](https://memorycircuits.hbsnzj.com/blog/7353107888.html)

Soak & Scrub Removable Parts

Soak grates, flavorizers and heat shields in warm, soapy water for 15–20 minutes.

Scrub thoroughly with a nylon brush, rinse and let air-dry.

If grease is baked on, add a tablespoon of baking soda to the water to help break it down.

Clean Burner Tubes & Orifices

Inspect each burner tube for blockages.

Run a pipe cleaner or small wire through each port; clear away any webs, rust flakes or nest debris.

Wipe down the exterior of the tubes with a damp cloth.

Inspect & Clean Hoses, Regulator & Valves

Check hoses for cracks, brittleness or chew marks—replace if damaged.

Remove the gas regulator and wipe it down.

Use soapy water to gently spray the regulator outlet and valve connections; wipe clean.

Neutralize Odors

If a musty mouse-nest smell lingers, mix a paste of baking soda and water.

Apply to interior surfaces, let sit 15 minutes, then wipe down with a damp cloth.

Best Practices for Prevention

Inspect and clean your grill at least once per season, ideally before your first barbecue.

Keep the grill covered when not in use to deter rodents and moisture buildup.

Store the propane tank upright on a flat surface, not indoors.

Use nontoxic rodent deterrent packets around your grill area (replace every 3 months).
