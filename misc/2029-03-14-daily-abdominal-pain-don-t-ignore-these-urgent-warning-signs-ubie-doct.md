What could be causing my daily abdominal pain?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Daily abdominal pain is often linked to stress, digestive issues, or conditions such as irritable bowel syndrome (IBS), food intolerances, acid reflux, or chronic constipation. In some cases, it may signal a more serious underlying issue like inflammatory bowel disease, ulcers, or gallbladder problems. Pinpointing the specific cause is essential for effective treatment and symptom relief.

Explanation

Abdominal pain that occurs daily can be due to various reasons. One common cause is stress, which can make the stomach hurt more often. When people feel stressed, their bodies might react by causing pain in the belly.

Another possible reason for daily stomach pain is digestive problems, such as irritable bowel syndrome (IBS). IBS is a condition that affects how the stomach and intestines work. It can cause pain, bloating, and changes in bathroom habits.
