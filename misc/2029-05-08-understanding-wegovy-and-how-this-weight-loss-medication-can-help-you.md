How Does Wegovy Impact Weight Loss?

Beyond its blood sugar regulating effects, GLP-1 receptor activation also slows gastric emptying in the intestines while increasing satiety signals through hypothalamic pathways.

The combination of these responses can work together to help increase your satiation levels from the foods you eat and act as an appetite suppressant to counter cravings.
