How to safely dispose of expired medications?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Expired medications should be disposed of through drug take-back programs, DEA-authorized collection sites, or FDA-approved home disposal products such as deactivation pouches. These methods safely neutralize drugs, preventing accidental ingestion, misuse, and environmental contamination. Flushing is only recommended for medications on the FDA's flush list, while most others can be mixed with unappealing substances like coffee grounds and sealed before trashing.

Expired medications should be disposed of properly to prevent harm to others and the environment. The best method is to use a drug take-back program, which allows you to return unwanted medicines to a designated location for safe disposal. These programs are often organized by local pharmacies, hospitals, or community organizations.

If a take-back program is not available, you can use special disposal products designed for home use. These products deactivate the medication, making it safe to throw away in the trash. Always follow the instructions on the disposal product to ensure safety.
