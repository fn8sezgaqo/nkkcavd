# Arnold Schwarzenegger Tells Spring Break Crowds: 'Stay. At. Home.'

Arnold Schwarzenegger knows that it's spring break, and he doesn't care. With the government advising social distancing as a preventative measure against the spread of COVID-19, he wants people to stop going to parties and stay the hell home.

[Arnold Schwarzenegger](https://lumina-dermis.sunsimiaosd.com/blog/4501441502.html)

In a video on Twitter, Schwarzenegger explained how he's passing the time while avoiding large gatherings, and reiterated how important it is to stop hanging out in public as if everything is normal.

[Spring Break Crowds Stay](https://7161777.com/uja5qacp2/)

[Arnold Schwarzenegger Tells](https://tissue-renewal.china-akan.com/blog/7993725469.html)

"Yeah, I'm at home, taking in jacuzzis, smoking a little stogy," he says. "I just finished a bike ride and did a little bit of a workout. And I just, you know, keep staying at home, away from the crowds and away from outside. The reason why I'm saying that is because I still see photographs and videos of people sitting at outside cafés all over the world, having a good time and hanging out in crowds. That is not wise. Because that is how you can get the virus. That's how you get it, by contact with other people."

"So stay away from crowds," he continues, "stay away from being in restaurants and outdoor cafés, especially now it's springtime when all the kids are going to the beach and celebrating and drinking and all that stuff. This is not a good idea. So just stay away from the crowds, go home, and then we can overcome this whole problem, this whole virus, in no time. But you've got to go and follow those orders. Just remember: stay at home, don't go into crowds."

Message delivered, Schwarzenegger signs off with a line from his movie Jingle All The Way: "Put that cookie down!"

It's the second PSA that Schwarzenegger has posted this week, having already shared a video in which he and his pet donkeys, Whiskey and Lulu, advocated for social distancing. This time around, his animal companion is his terrier, Cherry.
