# BFFs Ryan Reynolds and Hugh Jackman Are Teaming Up for 'Deadpool 3'

Rumors surrounding whether Hugh Jackman will return as Wolverine or be recast for the Marvel Cinematic Universe have been circulating for years now, ever since the rights to the -X-Men reverted to Marvel Studios. And now it's official: the Australian actor will be reprising the role of Logan in the upcoming Deadpool 3, the first Deadpool movie to take place in the MCU.

[Teaming Up](https://appetitecode.jcesqc8.com/blog/1895506762.html)

Deadpool star Ryan Reynolds made the announcement in a video which he shared on social media last night, in typical tongue-in-cheek fashion.

"I've had to really search my soul on this one," he said. "[Deadpool's] first appearance in the MCU obviously needs to feel special. We need to stay true to the character, find new depth and motivation and meaning. Every Deadpool needs to stand out and stand apart. It's been an incredible challenge that has forced me to reach down deep inside... and I have nothing. Yeah. Just completely empty up here."

"But we did have one idea," he continued, just as Hugh Jackman walked past him in the background of the video. "Hey, Hugh, want to play Wolverine one more time?" To which Jackman replied: "Yeah, sure Ryan."

Reynolds and Jackman first appeared alongside each other as Wade Wilson and Logan in the 2009 prequel X-Men Origins: Wolverine, in which Reynolds played a considerably different version of his character. Although technically, Jackman did also make a cameo in the first Deadpool movie, in which a disfigured Wade hides behind a magazine cover bearing the handsome actor's face.

The pair followed up that announcement with another irreverent video clip this morning, in which they claim they will answer some burning questions relating to the timeline of the movie, acknowledging that Jackman's character died at the end of 2017's Logan. Of course, now that they're both in the MCU, any plot details are kept in a locked vault, and so the entire explanation is drowned out by 'Wake Me Up Before You Go-Go' by Wham!, before ending with Jackman thanking Marvel president Kevin Feige for having him back.

Deadpool 3 will be released on 9th June 2024.
