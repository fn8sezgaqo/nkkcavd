# Justin Timberlake Was Pelted With Urine at a Charity Concert in 2003

Justin Timberlake is still pissed about being pelted with piss in 2003. During a Feb. 14 interview on the Graham Norton Show, Timberlake recalled dodging bottles of urine at a 2003 charity concert in front of 500,000 people. Now that’s determination to do good in the name of charity.

[Urine at Charity Concert](https://focus-and-calm.sunsimiaosd.com/)

On the late night show, Timberlake told the story of performing at Molson Canadian Rocks for Toronto, a Canadian charity event to help revive the country's economy after the devastating 2003 SARS epidemic. Mick Jagger casually called up JT and asked him to play at the event, which was organized by The Rolling Stones. And you don’t say no to Mick Jagger.

Timberlake agreed to the show and and rolled up to Toronto to do some good for charity.

“I looked at the bill and it’s The Rolling Stones and AC/DC and The Guess Who—you get the picture—and like somewhere in there: Me," he says. "I just remember saying to the band before going on stage, ‘I don’t think this is going to go well'.”

Call it a premonition. Timberlake was booed by the crowd throughout his first song, with the sea of people throwing muffins, toilet paper, and—wait for it—bottles of urine at the star. Timberlake recalled dodging the plastic bottles of pee while singing, refusing to give in to the haters.
