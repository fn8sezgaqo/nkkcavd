In Yellowstone, patriarch John Dutton (Kevin Costner) favors a softshell vest over a denim shirt. Brolin’s style in Outer Range reflects more of the Yellowstone bunkhouse, those who heard cattle and spend most of the day riding. Rip (Cole Hauser), the bunkhouse leader, wears a canvas military jacket over a button-up flannel shirt.

Brolin’s Abbott, unlike Dutton, dresses in a style closer to his children and ranchers, implying a different kind of character relationship; with a much smaller ranch than the Yellowstone, Abbott seems closer to the everyday duties of the land.
