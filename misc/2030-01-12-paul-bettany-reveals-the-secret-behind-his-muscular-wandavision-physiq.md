# Paul Bettany Reveals the Secret Behind His Muscular ‘WandaVision’ Physique

With the WandaVision finale just one day away, fans are gearing up to have some of their most burning questions answered. And in an interview on The Late Show with Stephen Colbert, Vision actor Paul Bettany just provided the solution to one mystery: namely, how a vibranium robot can look so damn good in knitwear.

When asked by Colbert whether that is his real chest on display or "digital wizardry," Bettany explained exactly how he achieves Vision's build.

"I'm going to get very specific with you now," he said. "About 42 inches of it is my chest, and about 8 inches just isn't. But it's not digital, it's a suit that you wear. They give you a muscle suit... It's not mine."

This revelation might come as a bit of a disappointment to a certain quotient of WandaVision viewers who were impressed with just how well Bettany filled out his variety of dad sweaters throughout the show's various sitcom-inspired eras—not to mention last week's emotional flashback scene. Forget the face that launched a thousand ships, these were the shoulders that finally got people to ship Vision and Wanda in the first place.

However, Bettany adds that just because Vision's synthezoid physique is achieved via costuming rather than the transformative workouts used by his MCU peers like Chris Hemsworth, doesn't mean he can eat whatever he wants.

Bettany kept working out with his trainer throughout the show's delayed filming schedule, in order to ensure he would be ready to step back into his super-suit (and the warped reality of Westview) at a moment's notice.

"We were all told 'Well, this is going to be a month, a month and a half, maybe two months,' and then it was three months," he told Men's Health earlier this year. "And the whole time, having to maintain the level of fitness and preparedness. It did happen, that when they called us back to work, we had less than two weeks notice. So that would’ve been a bummer if I’d put on 20 pounds. Keeping yourself motivated during all of that was tough, but I was lucky enough to have a trainer who, during the quarantine, we could Zoom and I could go out on the deck and he could take me through a workout."
