# Melisandre Wants You to Stop Complaining About the 'Game of Thrones' Finale

The Game of Thrones series finale premiered on this very day one year ago, and while most finales lead to some discussion and dissent amongst fans, it seems like the GoT finale disappointed just about everyone who watched it. One fan even went so far as to create a petition calling for a re-do to the ending, and as of right now it has 1.8 million signatures. And while some of the actors from the HBO show felt similarly about the finale (Emilia Clarke, we're looking at you), a good number of them stood behind the show and its writers, and Sophie Turner even called the petition "disrespectful."

Carice van Houten played Melisandre on the show, and she recently told Insider that she loved the finale.

"I loved the randomness and clumsiness of picking some guy, which is very representative of the world right now, as crazy people are ruling the world," van Houten said. "I thought it was a great comparison."

"The fact that some people were so disappointed is because everything before that was so good. So it feels a bit ungrateful. You've had such great times and then yeah, you're going to be disappointed because it's not going to go exactly how you anticipated," she added.

The actress also explained that while criticism of her character didn't bother her, she felt badly for the writers after the call for a re-do, calling it "scary."
