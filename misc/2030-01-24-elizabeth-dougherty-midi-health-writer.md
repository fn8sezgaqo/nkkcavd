Elizabeth Dougherty

Bio: Elizabeth Dougherty, MSJ, has been a writer and editor for more than 35 years. She enjoys writing about health and well-being, particularly women’s health, mental health, and lifestyle. She is passionate about providing accurate, evidence-based health information to help people make informed choices. Elizabeth has edited more than a hundred nonfiction books. She coauthored the book The Complete Expert-to-Author Guide to empower subject-matter experts, such as healthcare practitioners, to write books that can change lives. Her work has appeared on HealthCentral and BabyCenter, where her coverage of depression won two Digital Health Awards. A devoted yogi with a recently acquired meno belly, Elizabeth is now focused on upping her cardio.

Education: Master of Science in Journalism from Northwestern University, Bachelor of Arts in Journalism from Creighton University, Stanford University professional courses on publishing on the web and book publishing
