# Kevin Hart Just Recreated The Rock's Most Famous Outfit for Halloween

Kevin Hart's Halloween costume might look a little familiar. In a video he just posted to Instagram, Hart paid tribute to one of his friend Dwayne "The Rock" Johnson's best-known looks: the turtleneck, chain and fanny pack ensemble from the '90s that regularly does the rounds on the internet. Public opinion remains divided on whether it is an iconic fashion moment or an unconscionable one, but Hart's homage to the look was spookily accurate, right down to the feathery wig and his swaggering, lopsided pose.

In the video, Hart went trick-or-treating at Casa Johnson, only to be refused the giant-sized candy bars The Rock had just given out to the other kids, in favor of "bite-sized, tiny, mini" treats that reflected his diminutive stature. "Put this in your fanny pack," said The Rock.

The fact that Hart is throwing himself back into his long-running troll war with Johnson suggests he really is feeling more like himself after his car accident in September: he shared an update on his recovery this week, saying he felt "thankful" and ready for what the future holds.

The Halloween post wasn't just a gag between the two old buddies; it was also a low-key plug for their new movie, Jumanji: The Next Level. The young trick-or-treaters featured in the video are all in costume as the cast of the movie, and the post ends with a message advertising the imminent release of the new trailer.
