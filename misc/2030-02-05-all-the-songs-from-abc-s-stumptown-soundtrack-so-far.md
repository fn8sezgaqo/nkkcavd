# All the Songs From ABC’s ‘Stumptown’ Soundtrack So Far

Stumptown is ABC's new drama that stars Cobie Smulders.

It's a fun 'detective' show that doesn't have a boring moment.

The show's music is great—that's why you're here.

[Soundtrack So](https://protocols.peptidebiotechhgh.com/article/peptide-storage-and-handling-guide-for-research-labs-3ec95e228878/)

ABC's new series Stumptown isn't exactly re-inventing the wheel. But with Cobie Smulders leading the cast at her charismatic best (plus New Girl's Jake Johnson! As a bartender!), it's exactly the type of 'loose cannon private detective' show you need this fall.

One thing that stands out about Stumptown from the very first episode is the soundtrack that accompanies the on-screen adventures of Dex, Smulders' character. While much of the show's background, tone-setting, instrumental music is composed by Gabriel Mann and Rebecca Kneubuhl, there are a few moments early on when a song comes on, and you can't help but audibly go "ooh!" (you might never hear "Love Will Keep Us Together" the same way again). And what more could you ever want in a soundtrack than that?

Season 1, Episode 6

Episode 6 prominently featured a new character, a pop star named 'Fiona X,' who just so happened to be a former flame of Cobie Smulders' Dex. We couldn't include any Fiona X on the list this week because, well, Fiona X is a fictional character (though her music on the show sounded great!). That being said, the actress who plays her, Ioanna Gika, is actually a popular indie musician whose music has even been positively reviewed by Pitchfork. Below you can check out a song from her solo career, and another from her time with Io Echo, her band.
