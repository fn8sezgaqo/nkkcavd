# Tabitha Tate, Midi Health

[Tate Midi Health](https://focus-and-calm.sunsimiaosd.com/blog/0345959706.html)

Tabitha Tate, MSN, APRN, FNP-C

The road to Midi: “I’ve been practicing medicine since 2018, building a diverse foundation in primary care, women’s health, aesthetics, and hospice care. My journey started in labor and delivery, where I developed my passion for women’s health. I joined Midi because it allowed me to focus on my favorite parts of care: listening, education, and building relationships.”

Care, like family: “When my sister was diagnosed with breast cancer in her early 30s, I became her support person, experiencing the patient side of care for the first time. That journey changed me. Now, I approach every patient as if they’re a loved one. I prioritize slowing down, building trust, and ensuring they feel educated and involved in their care.”

Grounded in education: “I love teaching women about their health, especially lifestyle-based options for care and sexual health. That’s why I strive to create a safe space where all patients feel comfortable asking vulnerable questions and sharing concerns. I love being a guide who empowers them to take charge of their health with knowledge and confidence.”
