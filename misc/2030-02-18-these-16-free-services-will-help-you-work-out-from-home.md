# These 16 Free Services Will Help You Work Out from Home

As the coronavirus pandemic spreads in the U.S., many people are wisely limiting their contact and staying indoors to abide by "social distancing" principles. Many states have even taken steps to close business or issue 'shelter in place' orders—which means that just as many people are also worried that they won’t be able to keep up with their usual workout routines.

It would be irresponsible to go to the gym right now, whether it's open or not—but that doesn't mean that you can't still get a good workout in at home. We have plenty of at-home workouts for you to choose from to help you stay fit (as well as our #MHDailyChallenge on Instagram). And some of our favorite trainers, like Don Saladino and Bobby Maximus, are offering their own free bodyweight programs (find those here and here, respectively).

That's not all: some gyms, like Gold's and Planet Fitness, as well as studios and other streaming video services have started to offer online workouts for free. Whether you're looking for a quick sweat or trying to stick to your usual training, here's a list of free services you can use to help you stay active during the coronavirus.

All Out Studio

Get a free 30-day trial for All Out Studio, the exclusive app for Men's Health's online training programs, when you use the promo code MHFREE30. To redeem, just follow these steps:

- Navigate to www.alloutstudio.com

- Click “Start Free Trial”

- Create an account and select monthly subscription

- Enter the promo code MHFREE30.

You are now in! To access on-demand workouts, download the All Out Studio app on iOS, Android or Apple TV, and use the same login credentials to get started.

Samsung Smart TV

Good news for those who own a Samsung Smart TV—you now have access to 5,000 hours of free workouts from the comfort of your own home. TV-goers will have access to over 250 instructional classes across six different apps (barre3, Calm, Echelon, Fitplan, Jillian Michaels Fitness and obé Fitness), giving millions of people the chance to get in shape, without all the fees that go into paying for a gym membership.

Centr

If you’re looking to get shredded like one of your favorite Avengers, then you’re in luck. Centr, the fitness and wellness app created by Chris Hemsworth, is offering a free six-week membership, plus access to his team of experts. The offer is only available until the end of the month, so you need to sign up fast to get your workout with Thor.

Planet Fitness

To help those stuck at home, Planet Fitness will now be offering “Home Work-Ins,” a series of free online classes led by the fitness company’s certified trainers. The 20-minute classes will be live-streamed daily at 7PM EST on the gym’s official Facebook page, with the chance to watch a repeat of the broadcast on their official YouTube channel. The best part? The classes will be using no equipment, so no need to scramble around for the same weights you use at the gym.

FitOn

If you’re looking to have some inspiration from your favorite celebrity or trainer to help you train at-home, this is a good app to use. With customized workouts to help you achieve your fitness goals, you could also do workouts with the likes of Gabrielle Union, Jeanette Jenkins, and more to help you break a sweat without leaving the comfort of your living room.

C25K 5K Trainer

Just because 5Ks and marathons are being cancelled doesn’t mean that you still can’t train for one online. This super easy set up has you running up to a half-hour three times a week, giving you more stamina and the ability to complete your own 5K while stuck at home. You also have your own personal trainer cheering you on as you get better and better.

Nike Training Club

For those looking to continue their workout game with one of the top sports companies in the world, this is a good app for you. The app provides custom workouts and custom timeframes based on your individual fitness needs and gives you the option to work out with some of your favorite athletes, such as Isaiah Thomas and Cristiano Ronaldo. Plus, it comes with an Apple Watch option for those who prefer to not use their cell phones when working out.

Gold's Gym

This popular chain is letting users use its app for free until May 31, providing a motivating and results-driven experience that you can do from the comfort of your own home. With over 600 workouts for both indoors and outdoors, you have an endless number of workout opportunities any time of the day.

Strong by Zumba

If you’re into a different element of working out than using weights and barbells, then Strong by Zumba might be the answer for your fitness needs. Using combinations synced to music to give you an all-around workout, this will give you a workout without needing much or any equipment. You can even work out with celebrity trainers like Erin Oprea to get the physique you strive for.

Adidas Training by Runtastic

This app provides you with bursts of short workouts so that you can perform your routine any time, anywhere. There’s also the option of deciding how many times a week you are looking to train, and the app helps you create a plan based on your bodyweight, fitness level and your general availability to work out.

Zwift

This app is perfect for those who prefer running or spinning as their form of exercise. With various computerized places, you have the opportunity to run through the streets of New York or bike your way through the streets of London. You can even join various app users in group rides or runs, boosting your endurance and not feeling so isolated in your training.

Retro Fitness

As one of the biggest fitness chains in the country, Retro Fitness is helping gym-goers by offering free livestream classes everyday on its Facebook page. Taught by expert trainers, these classes will be offered Monday through Friday at 6PM EST, with everyone—member or non-member—having access to their routines.
