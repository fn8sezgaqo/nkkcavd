LIKE PLENTY OF scruffy dudes who read “Into the Wild” at an impressionable age, I’ve camped all over the U.S. From the deserts of the Southwest to the forests of the Northeast, I’ve pitched tents in dry heat, humid rains, and the occasional Walmart parking lot when every other option failed. Through it all, I’ve learned to sacrifice the comforts of daily life. You trade a mattress and crisp sheets for a cramped sleeping bag, give up hot showers and celebrate any running water, while settling for a barely functional toilet. But one thing I’ve never given up is a skincare routine.

Sure, it’s not the multi-step ritual I follow at home, but skincare matters just as much outdoors—arguably more. Out here, the routine gets stripped down to the essentials. No serums, no toners, no eye creams—just the basics that keep your skin clean and protected. Camping is about getting back to basics anyway, and honestly, your skin doesn’t mind the break.

When I’m packing my camping toiletries kit, I focus on two things: multipurpose use and unscented products. In an ideal scenario, you’re at a site with a bathroom and shower—but even then, hot water is rare. You’ll want to keep it quick. And since most still run on that quarter-per-minute system, I don’t exactly have buckets of change lying around.

None of that excuses skipping shampoo and conditioner. You still need to wash your hair—especially if you’re hiking or doing anything that leaves you sweaty. Chances are you’re also packing lighter than you would for a hotel trip, which means downsizing your Dopp kit (or ditching it altogether). Multi-use products solve the problem, cutting down what you need to cram in your bag.

Then it’s time to ditch the scents. I love cologne as much as the next grooming editor, but the outdoors isn’t the place for fragrance—it attracts wildlife you don’t want around. Best case, bugs swarm you. Worst case, something much bigger decides you smell like dinner. Go fragrance-free and save yourself the hospital bill. This is everything I pack in my toiletry kit when camping.
