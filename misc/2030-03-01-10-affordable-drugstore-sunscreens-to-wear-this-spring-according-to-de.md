Skincare can seem complicated, but you know what isn’t: sunscreen. Wearing sunscreen is non-negotiable. Every single person needs to wear sunscreen every day, according to the American Academy of Dermatology (AAD). Not only does it help prevent skin cancer, which men are especially at risk for, but it also helps prevent signs of aging, dark spots and other skin issues that can be linked to sun damage. Basically, if you’re going to put one thing on your skin, it should be sunscreen.

The AAD also recommends using sunscreen that is broad spectrum, meaning it protects against both UVA and UVB rays, and with a minimum of SPF 30. You know what they don’t have guidelines for? Price. Sure, you can splurge on an expensive sunscreen, especially if you find one you like enough to wear every day, but a good sunscreen doesn’t have to take a huge chunk out of your wallet. Your local drugstore—or favorite shopping website—is a treasure trove of budget-friendly sunscreens. We’ve done the legwork and picked the very best, so if you’re unclear where to start or are overwhelmed by options, this list is your best jumping off point. And remember, a sunscreen won’t do any good if it’s not on your skin, so the best option is one you’ll actually wear.
