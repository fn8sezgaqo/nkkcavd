What To Consider

Hair Type

For fine or thin hair, hair brushes with tighter bristles are better since they can easily smooth and detangle. For thick hair, opt for brushes with bristles that are farther apart because they can better detangle and run through the hair without damaging it.

Bristle Material

The bristles on hair brushes are often made naturally or synthetically. Natural bristles typically come from the hair of boars; they can distribute your hair’s natural scalp oils evenly and give it a natural shine, and sometimes even increase blood flow by stimulating your scalp. And because these bristles are very close together, they’re great for fine or thin hair. On the other hand, synthetic bristles are often made with nylon and are often more spaced out, meaning they’re better for thick, coarse hair. Nylon bristle hair brushes can glide through pretty much any hair texture, making them versatile for styling and excellent at detangling.

Hairstyle

For styling your hair, dense, boar bristle hair brushes are considered better for holding and setting hair for curls or your preferred hairstyle. Other than that, some hair brushes are meant for styling long hair versus short hair. Be mindful of that before you buy.

Why You Should Use a Hair Brush

No matter your hair type—but especially if you have long or thick hair—hair brushes for men are essential. They're great for dispersing your hair products and natural hair oils evenly (the latter can prevent dry ends, frizzing, breakage, etc.), and setting the basic structure of your hairstyle. A good hair brush like can also massage your scalp to stimulate blood flow. If you've got curls and tangles, hair brushes can tame them better than curls. Lastly, hair brushes can remove hair that is ready to shed.

What Brush is Best for Thinning Hair?

Every hair type has an ideal hair brush, and if you're having more bad hair days than good, you brush may be to blame. A soft bristle brush is best for thinning hair because it gently detangles without pulling out your hair at the root. Boar hair is similar in structure to human hair and both contain keratin, which is the protein that forms the strength of your hair. A soft, boar bristle brush helps distribute your scalp oils to the rest of your hair, which encourages healthy hair growth.

How to Brush Your Hair?

Men should be brushing their hair every day, especially if you regularly apply product to your roots and ends. You want to brush in long, even strokes using light to moderate pressure. If you have tangles at the ends of your hair, start by brushing the lengths first before brushing all the way through from the roots. Brushing isn't so much about brushing out the lengths but about massaging the scalp. If you want healthy hair that grows long and strong, start with a healthy scalp. Gentle daily brushing will help maintain scalp health by increasing circulation to the scalp and distributing those natural oils from root to tip.
