When does The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes take place?

The film and book take place during the 10th Hunger Games. The original Hunger Games trilogy takes place during the 74th Hunger Games—so much, much later.

How is The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes connected to The Hunger Games?

The prequel takes place during the 10th Hunger Games and follows a character fans will be familiar with, Coriolanus Snow (played in the original films by Donald Sutherland). But rather than the tyrannical dictator he's later known as in the original four movies, The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes follows an 18-year-old Snow (Tom Blyth) before he became president.

[Ballad of Songbirds and](https://mendstride.snaketongueofficial.com/)

As a member of a failing noble family, Snow is tasked with restoring them to glory. During the 10th Hunger Games, Snow is paired with the female tribute from District 12, Lucy Gray (Rachel Zegler). The prequel follows their close relationship as Snow tries to help Lucy win the games, while both try to pursue their own personal goals. The story delves into how Coriolanus Snow became the villain we later see in the original trilogy, and how the games were the catalyst for his political ambitions.

The prequel is also where we learn more about the origins of "The Hanging Tree" song, which Katniss sings in Mockingjay. The song was actually first composed and sung by Lucy Gray. The song first refers to Arlo Chance, but later refers to Snow. The song is later considered a form of rebellion, hence Katniss' desire to sing it in the future.

Does The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes mention Katniss?

Nope, sorry. Anyone looking for a callout about Katniss or Peeta or anyone from the original trilogy will be disappointed. There is a small mention of Katniss, but it's the plant she's named after that's brought up in conversation, not the person herself.
