That's when I began journaling on a few questions: "What would I want to do, have, and be if I had $10 million in the bank?" and "What's my real target monthly income?" For the latter, in other words, how much would my dream life really cost if I paid on a monthly basis? (See fourhourworkweek.com/tmi.)
