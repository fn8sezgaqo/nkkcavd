# Check Out This Weekend's Official MH Fest Lineup

There is no health without mental health. One in 5 American adults experiences some form of mental illness in any given year, which means it’s highly likely that you or someone you love has been affected. And in the midst of the COVID-19 pandemic that is presenting a wide array of challenges for people across the country, it’s time—now more than ever—to truly focus on the importance of mental health.

That’s why Men’s Health is partnering with the National Alliance on Mental Illness (NAMI), the nation's largest grassroots mental health organization, to produce MH Fest, a 2-day virtual music festival featuring a lineup of exciting and dynamic performers. On Saturday, May 2 and Sunday, May 3, over 20 musicians will play music on the Men’s Health Instagram Live to help fundraise for NAMI, which is dedicated to building better lives for the millions of Americans affected by mental illness.

The artists playing MH Fest run the gamut in terms of style and genre: rock, pop, dance, jazz, folk hip-hop, R&B, folk, country, and more. Plus, these musicians have been outspoken about mental health via advocacy, interviews, and their music. Before you tune in this weekend, get acquainted with the MH Fest lineup by checking out this official Spotify playlist.

Should you choose to donate, your contribution will go directly to education programs, advocacy, and support for helping those affected by mental illness live healthy, fulfilling lives supported by a community that cares.

You can donate to NAMI at this link: http://donate.nami.org/mhfest

Thank you for your support and enjoy the show!
