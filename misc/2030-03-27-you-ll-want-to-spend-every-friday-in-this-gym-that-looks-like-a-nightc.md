If you want to take your Friday night happy hour to the next level, don’t hit the club—hit the gym: Every weekend, stressed New Yorkers gather together for Noho Nights, a sweat session designed to help you blow off steam the way your typical bar night would, according to a New York Post report. The class takes place at Barry’s Bootcamp, but make no mistake, it doesn’t look like your average steel gray gym. With red lights beaming and EDM beats bumping, you’d think you were at any other raging nightclub in the city.

Thirty-one-year-old trainer Matty Maggiacomo and his 29-year-old DJ boyfriend Aaron Aanenson started the class. While Aanenson performs a set, Maggiacomo puts the class through a hellish, hour-long, full-body workout with a mix of cardio and high-intensity interval training.

The two know what they’re doing: Research has shown that fast music can actually improve your athletic performance. And if you hit the gym regularly, you know a bangin’ workout playlist can keep you motivated throughout a grueling workout.

When the class first opened its doors, only 13 people showed up. Now it consistently fills up every Friday after the sign-up sheet goes live at noon—and there’s usually a waitlist.

Other NYC gyms are following the trend, like Overthrow Boxing Club, which offers a boxing class followed by a round of beers. The gym—adorned with neon lights and graffiti—is going for a “happy hour fight-club environment,” owner Joey Goodwin told the New York Post. Boxing and brews? Sounds like our kind of party.
