# Why Ilsa Faust—and Not Ethan Hunt—Is the Best Part of ‘Mission: Impossible’

[and Not Ethan](https://peplawllc.com/)

The following story contains spoilers for Mission: Impossible — Dead Reckoning Part One.

[Best Part of](https://calmvector.dns0663.com/)

ILSA FAUST, we hardly knew you. I’m using my grief as a vessel to honor Rebecca Ferguson’s one-of-a-kind Mission: Impossible character, who could easily be labeled as the best thing that’s happened to the entire franchise (Henry Cavill’s tight pants in Fallout, the sixth M:I installment, comes in at a close second, but that’s a totally different conversation. And one I’m not prepared to have at this very moment.)

Why has this woman become iconic, you ask? Why does she manage to serve in every single scene she’s in? What makes her Mother?

Ilsa Faust met her maker in Mission: Impossible — Dead Reckoning Part One, the first half of a two-part action adventure, but her untimely passing at the hands of the villainous Gabriel (Esai Morales) isn’t actually all that much of a surprise. She’s been booked and busy 144 levels below ground (Silo) and weaponizing her words with ‘The Voice’ (Dune and Dune: Part Two) for months now—a girl’s gotta eat—so, it’s only fair that the woman gets some serious R&R time. It just unfortunate that time will come at the expense of an appearance in what will likely be the most ambitious Mission: Impossible yet, in 2024’s Dead Reckoning Part Two.

[of Mission Impossible](https://thymosin-report.hbsnzj.com/blog/7654746610.html)

Nevertheless, her death still hurts like a quick stab to the heart. Which is, that’s right, what happened to her character.

When we first meet the former MI6 intelligence operative in Mission: Impossible — Rogue Nation, she’s a woman of few words. We think she’s about to fuck Ethan Hunt’s (Tom Cruise) shit up, removing her fashionable black suede heels—Ilsa doesn’t charge for feet pics, y'all—in what we assume is her pre-torture prep. But alas, she’s just playin' around with no real means to injure our leading man. Providing Hunt with a means of escape via key before chucking mallets and tossing guys twice her size around with her outlandishly powerful thighs (very Xenia Onatopp meets Natasha Romanoff, I must say), Ilsa reveals she’s under very deep cover within terrorist organization The Syndicate, and not an actual member.

Ilsa continues being the definition of a badass sultry could-kill-you-with-one-punch femme fatale chameleon: take your pick, from shooting a sniper rifle whilst giving in a stunning one-shoulder yellow evening gown, to curving along the hills of Morocco on a sleek motorcycle at incredibly high speeds (oh, and impaling an oafish man nicknamed ‘The Bone Doctor’ in a shadowed cat-and-mouse knife fight. The woman’s got skills like no other). Truly, we need a prequel spin-off to learn who raised this girl.

[Part of Mission Impossible](https://beautybiometrics.hbsnzj.com/)

Her presence remains strong going into Rogue Nation’s follow-up, officially inserting herself as a mainstay in the pre-established IMF gang of Ethan, Luther (Ving Rhames), and Benji (Simon Pegg). She's one of the gang, and we love to see it.

Her strong personality is put on full display, and she commands every scene she's in. Setting it up to have her replace Ethan Hunt is what director Christopher McQuarrie probably should've done, but we can't go back in time now can we?

Ilsa's courageousness remains unmatched, and as we see in Dead Reckoning Part One, she’s not afraid of death—especially if losing her life means saving someone else’s.

I’ll miss the quick delivery of her snarky quips in that soft raspy British tone of hers. I’ll miss the natural flips of her hair without any outside influence of wind. I’ll miss her ability to annihilate a room full of men without ever hinting at a call for backup.

[Not Ethan Hunt Is](https://brainmessenger.xianfengchem.com/)

Her death won’t be in vain, as Hunt will clearly use his boiling anger to avenge his latest love interest (he moves fast, though, as seen by the chemistry between him and franchise newcomer Hayley Atwell) in Dead Reckoning Part Two. But his goal will be a simple one: tear Gabriel apart, limb from limb.

Is there any hope that maybe Ilsa will return in flashbacks? Or Ilsa’s twin sister Lilsa (obviously that’s her name), also a MI6 agent because it runs in the Faust family, shows up to bring justice for her long lost sibling? Maybe she’s running a long con, tricking everyone—even Ethan—into thinking that she’s passed just to pull one over under the deepest cover she’s ever been in. Unlikely, but whatever. That’s fine. Nothing will bring Ilsa back, and instead of hoping for a return that’ll never happen, it’s best to think of the memories that’ve been made thus far.

Ilsa Faust changed Mission: Impossible forever, and for that, I will be forever grateful.R.I.P, Ilsa. You ate that.
