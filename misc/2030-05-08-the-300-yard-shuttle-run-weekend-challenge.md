# The 300-Yard Shuttle Run Weekend Challenge

Football is one of the world's most physically demanding sports, so it's no surprise that NFL players need to pass a gauntlet of conditioning tests during training camp. One of the most grueling tests—and your weekend fitness challenge—is the 300-yard shuttle run.

[Shuttle Run Weekend](https://7161777.com/zerad1/)

"It's a marker for speed and stamina," says Todd Durkin, C.S.C.S., creator of the 28-Day Fat Torch. Compared with traditional tests of endurance—like a 2-mile run—it better measures the type of staying power that's required for most sports. The better you score, the bigger your edge when chasing down an opponent, sprinting for the finish line, or driving hard for the last shot.

[Run Weekend Challenge](https://clinical.peppotom.com/article/b-type-natriuretic-peptide-tube-b-type-natriuretic-peptide-tube-demystified-core-principles-of-molecular-stability-traits-peptide-share-4a88362ec0a5/)

Durkin has seen elite athletes Drew Brees, Darren Sproles, and LaDainian Tomlinson run the 300-yard shuttle in world-class times of less than 60 seconds. Of course, most guys won't clock that, so he says to aim for anything less than 1:20.
