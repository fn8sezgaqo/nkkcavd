# The 5 Best Moments From Arnold Schwarzenegger’s Fitness Festival

Arnold Schwarzenegger attended the Arnold Sports Festival in Melbourne, Australia this past weekend

[From Arnold Schwarzenegger Fitness](https://insulin-pathways.hbsnzj.com/)

The event attracts everyone from bodybuilders to Mas-wrestlers, dancers, cup stackers, and pole dancers

Here are some of the highlights from Schwarzenegger's fitness-filled weekend

Chess players, dancers, wrestlers and bodybuilders have way more in common than you think. At least, they do when it comes to partying at the Arnold Sports Festival in Australia.

Over the weekend, an estimated 10,000 professional and amateur athletes made the pilgrimage to Melbourne to take part in the nation's largest multi-sport fitness event. Of course, the man himself, Arnold Schwarzenegger, was on hand for the celebrations too.

The event has made a major transformation from its early days of simply presenting the Strongman and bodybuilding competitions and now includes a few of what appear to be Arnold’s new favorite events: Mas wrestling, dance competitions, cup stacking, pole dancing (it’s a legitimate sport), and chess events. Perhaps most surprisingly, it appears the chess event is closest to Schwarzenegger’s heart.

“When you’re playing chess, you cannot think about anything else otherwise you get beaten,” he told News.com.au. “You have to stay focused every second. It’s a really great form of disconnection from everything else.”

Meeting the Women of Dance Revolution

The former Governor of California couldn’t help but get in on the dance party and take a selfie with the women of Dance Revolution. Hey guys: You can get a great workout in from dancing, too.

Bringing Attention to Mas Wrestling

We’re a little convinced Arnold is just using this event to take shameless selfies, but that’s OK, as long as he keeps bringing attention to sports like Mas wrestling. Mas wrestling, a sport that hails from Russia, involves both competitors holding onto a stick in a tug-of-war style match that tests an athlete’s endurance and skill. Though training for Mas wrestling certainly takes some expertise, you can start by increasing your agility now with these 15 drills you can do in 10 square feet.
