# Build Massive Shoulders in No time With These 3 Tough Moves

Compound exercises—squats, deadlifts, presses, rows—are the cornerstone of every great weightlifting routine. But we love isolation work that really carves muscle and makes you look like marble. And there's one area where isolation exercises can really pay off: shoulders. Large, toned shoulders can really fill a tee-shirt out and make you look truly impressive.

So we love this isolation sequence that Dan Flores, the CEO of Fitlink, showed us. It’s relatively simple, but incredibly challenging.

[No time](https://3899241.com/2i4oadvade/)

“I’m going to show you three shoulder moves when you’re crunched on time and you still want to build muscle,” he says. Indeed, you could use this sequence as an after-burner to your workout so you won’t tax your shoulders for the big moves.

Start with a series of front raises, in a neutral grip. “Make sure you stop and start with control,” says Flores. When you’re working shoulders, technique is important for keeping injury at bay. This move works your anterior deltoids, or the front muscles of your shoulder.

[Shoulders in](https://copperglow.dns0663.com/)

[Shoulders in No time](https://incretin-path.escortskhi.com/)

Next, move onto lateral raises, bent slightly forward. Here, you’re working your lateral deltoids, the muscles on the side of your shoulder.

Lastly, move immediately to bent-over raises, flaring your arms out. This will help build your posterior delts, on the muscles on the back of your shoulder.

[Build Massive Shoulders in](https://bioreg.wiki/t740f5/)

Try for 15 reps of each move, and do this sequence three times. You’ve got a complete shoulder workout in basically no time at all.

Three times not too easy? Then Flores issues a challenge: “If you can do 3, try to beat my goal of 7.” No problem, right, guys?
