Sometimes when a cyst appears, dermatologists advise patients to simply wait it out —often, they'll go away on their own. But in her latest Instagram video, Dr. Pimple Popper—aka, dermatologist and TLC host Dr. Sandra Lee, MD—finds one patient for whom that definitely wasn't the case.

As the clip begins, Dr. Lee asks the man how long he's been dealing with what appears to be a large steatocystoma, and the man replies that he's been living with the growth for the better part of twenty years. Thankfully for him, that changed quickly during his visit: "You're going to be sad to see it go," Dr. Lee jokes, before making quick work of the steatocystoma, draining it with a hard pinch and revealing, as she puts it, "a pretty, clarified butter kind of thing."

Steatocystomas—often referred to in the context of a steatocystoma multiplex—are typically small, benign lumps that are filled with sebum, a yellowish, oily substance that helps lubricate the hair, skin, and nails, according to the National Institute of Health. In the clip, Dr. Lee also spends some time removing the cyst's sac wall, a step that will help lower the risk of the cyst returning.
