Tesamorelin (10mg)

Tesamorelin is a synthetic growth hormone-releasing hormone analog that triggers the secretion of growth hormone. It is typically prescribed to people with a deficiency of growth hormone and has been used extensively in the treatment of visceral adiposity in human immunodeficiency virus (HIV) infected patients with lipodystrophy (1). It’s also been shown to reduce the size of carotid-intima media size (cIMT) and thus reduce the progression of carotid atherosclerotic vascular disease (2). Patients are generally prescribed 2 mg daily given by subcutaneous injection for treatment, with some studies prolonging treatment for up to 12 months or longer.

While we do not endorse the consumption of research chemicals, we’ve read that bodybuilders and athletes typically use Tesamorelin to enhance muscle growth and accelerate fat loss, specifically around the waist.

PT-141 (Bremelanotide) (10mg)

PT-141 is a unique peptide in that its key use is in improving sexual function in both women and men. It works by binding with melanocortin receptors which stimulate the hypothalamus, thus resulting in action potentials being sent to the anterior pituitary gland. This leads to better erections and increased sex drive.

Current research suggests that PT-141 may serve as an alternative treatment for ED as it possesses a high erectogenic potential as well as a highly favorable tolerability profile and results in significant erections in patients who do not have an adequate response to a PDE5 inhibitor (3). In female patients, it’s been shown to increase sexual desire and arousal as well as decrease distress associated with sexual dysfunction (4).

Male patients have been prescribed doses ranging from 0.3 to 10 mg (though generally it’s closer to 4 to 6 mg) depending on the study and female patients have been prescribed 1.25 to 1.75 mg.
