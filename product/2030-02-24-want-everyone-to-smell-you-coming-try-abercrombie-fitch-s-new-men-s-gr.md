# Want Everyone To Smell You Coming? Try Abercrombie & Fitch’s New Men’s Grooming Products

Have you ever wanted your face and beard to smell like the inside of a medium-sized polo tee that's been washed with a blend of pine needles and '90s nostalgia?

If so, good news! Sporting-goods-emporium-turned-faux-bro-staple Abercrombie & Fitch recently released a men's grooming line developed by the company's in-house personal care team.

According to a press release, "The new line covers each stage of a man's facial shaving routine, in a four step kit, making the grooming routine an easy one stop shop." Everything apparently has a "fresh and woody aroma" — and while we haven't smelled this stuff ourselves, we're guessing it's something along the lines of that A&F cologne that hits you like a ton of bricks as soon as you set foot in a suburban mall.

The good news? At least everything is super affordable. The very, very bad news? Everyone will most certainly smell you coming from a mile away.
