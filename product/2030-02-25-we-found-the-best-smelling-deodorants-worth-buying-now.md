This type of memory is incredibly resistant to interference, meaning it’s no surprise a smell, like the scent of the best smelling deodorants for men, which we’re going to talk about here, can take you back to a place in your brain you thought was long gone. We’re talking about Dad’s go-to aftershave, cookies in Grandma’s kitchen, the freshly cut grass before a Saturday morning soccer game. One scent memory that I’d wager many of us wish we could forget, though, is the stench of not so sweet smelling deodorant wafting toward the ceiling in the middle school locker room (or any room inhabited by a group of middle schoolers). For me, it was a drugstore body spray. I shudder at the thought.

What to Look for

Sweat reduction: Deodorant is, first and foremost, a personal product. If you’re looking for sweat reduction, opt for an antiperspirant/deodorant combo. Remember that deodorants only control odor, not sweat.

Scent: Once you’ve decided on a deodorant, look for one with a scent you like. A stronger scent usually means more long lasting protection. Feel free to mix it up or stick with one all the time, you can't go wrong either way.

[Buying Now](https://7161777.com/7rr7ku/)

Non-irritating ingredients: If you’re prone to skin irritation, you may want to consider a baking soda-free version as baking soda has been shown to cause irritation in some people. Look instead for ingredients like magnesium, tapioca starch and arrowroot powder.

Consistency type: Finally, you’ll want to consider the delivery mechanism. Most deodorants come in solid stick form: chalkier ones may carry more risk of leaving stains on clothes. Sprays, gels and lotions are also available if you’re not into the stick thing.
