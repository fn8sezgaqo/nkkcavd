If you want better reflexes, you need to teach your entire body to react instinctively. The key is to use high-speed drills that simultaneously engage your brain and your muscles, a combination that forces you to move quicker and more efficiently. Try this simple reaction game called "box tag," which requires a partner and allows you to enhance your movement skills while quickly reacting to visual stimuli. To perform the exercise, create two 10' x 10' squares next to each other using cones, rocks, or whatever is on hand. Stand in the middle of one box while your partner stands in the middle of the other. Face each other, and have your partner make quick, intentional movements within his box while you mirror his every move. Go for 10 to 20 seconds, rest for a few seconds, and repeat -- only this time, you lead the exercise. Do this 6 to 10 times a session, twice a week.
