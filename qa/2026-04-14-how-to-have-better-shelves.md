# How To Have Better Shelves

Putting up shelves is a man’s perfect weekend project. It gives you a place to put stuff. You can do it in two days. And you already have a level, a pencil, and a drill. What you don’t have is inspiration, so let's fix that. Here are shelf ideas of every type, so you can impress friends, display your memorabilia, or just wander around with a snifter of cognac in one hand, brushing a finger along the spines of your first editions.

Not impressed by any of these projects? Want something more appropriate for your castle in the Scottish highlands? Go ahead and build yourself a bookcase that someone will lean on one day, only to have it creak open and reveal the misty stairs down to the cavern where you keep your sea plane. But really, you could do this with any door on sturdy hinges. And if you edge each shelf with a piece of trim, the books won’t go flying off it—and you won’t have to pay for those fake book fronts that are a dead giveaway.

Start making your lumber shopping list, and don’t forget to drill into the studs. Oh, and when you’re done, take a shelfie.
