Cantaloupe

Cantaloupe is one of the most vitamin C-rich foods available. A single slice of cantaloupe can provide upwards of 25mg of vitamin C.

Oranges

Most of the time, when we think of vitamin C, we likely think of oranges. Oranges are an amazing source of vitamin C, with both the fruit itself as well as natural fruit juices containing a high concentration of vitamin C.

Grapefruit

Second on the list of citrus fruits that contain a good amount of vitamin C is grapefruit. A single medium-sized grapefruit can provide around 56 mg of vitamin C, making it an excellent addition to any breakfast. Grapefruit is also known to be an excellent diet food.

Broccoli

One of the foods that many of us despised as children happens to be a great source of vitamin C as well as a host of other nutrients like iron, potassium, calcium, and zinc; broccoli is a must-have addition to any dinner plate to improve the number of nutrients you are getting.

Another food that kids and adults claim to despise is the brussel sprout. Like other cruciferous vegetables, Brussel sprouts are a major source of multiple vitamins. If you can tolerate a few servings every so often, it’ll be great for your health.

Cabbage

Cabbage is another vegetable that has a whole range of nutrients, including large amounts of vitamin C. We didn’t make the distinction here, but depending on the type of cabbage, you’ll get different values of nutrients.
