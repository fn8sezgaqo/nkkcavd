Receding hairlines in women can happen due to different types of hair loss. One common type is androgenetic alopecia, which is a genetic condition that can affect women as well as men. Another type is frontal fibrosing alopecia, which specifically affects the front hairline in women.

Androgenetic Alopecia

This type of hair loss is influenced by hormones and genetics.

It can lead to thinning hair all over the scalp or a receding hairline.

Treatment options may include medications that can help slow down hair loss.
