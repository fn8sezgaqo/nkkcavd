Rapid abdominal distension, or a quickly swelling belly, can happen due to a few different medical problems.

[distension Ubie Doctor's Note](https://peplawllc.com/)

Blockages: Sometimes, there can be a blockage in the stomach or intestines. This can cause the belly to swell quickly and might also cause pain.

Cancer: Certain types of cancer, like lymphoma, can also cause the belly to swell rapidly. This happens because the cancer can grow fast and affect the organs inside the belly.

If someone experiences rapid abdominal distension, it is important to seek medical help to find out the cause and get the right treatment.
