Spasms under the right ribs often occur because the rib cage plays a key role in supporting your spine. When back or rib muscles are weak, imbalanced, or strained, they can twitch or cramp along the lower right ribs. Strengthening your back and rib muscles, improving posture, and stretching regularly may help prevent these spasms from returning.

However, spasms under the right ribs can also point to other underlying issues, such as gallbladder problems, liver conditions, nerve irritation, or digestive concerns. Because the causes vary widely, guessing isn't the safest approach. Explanation

Muscle spasms under the ribs can be uncomfortable. One reason for these spasms might be related to how your rib cage helps keep your spine steady. If your rib cage or spine isn't strong enough, it could lead to spasms.

How the Rib Cage Works

The rib cage is important for keeping your spine in place.

If the rib cage is not stable, it might cause muscle spasms.
