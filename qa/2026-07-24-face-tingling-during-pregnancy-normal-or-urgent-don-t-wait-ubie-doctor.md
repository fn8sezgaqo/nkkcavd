Internal Medicine

Face tingling during pregnancy is relatively common and often linked to hormonal changes, fluid retention, nerve compression, or vitamin deficiencies. However, tingling in the face can sometimes signal more serious conditions like preeclampsia, gestational diabetes, or a pinched nerve. If the tingling is persistent, one-sided, or paired with symptoms such as swelling, headaches, vision changes, dizziness, or weakness, it's essential to contact your doctor promptly.

Face tingling during pregnancy may be due to various reasons, including nerve issues. According to the references provided, facial nerve problems can sometimes occur. One study mentions facial nerve palsy, which is a condition that can lead to weakness or tingling in the face. Another reference discusses Bell's palsy, which can be associated with preeclampsia, a serious condition during pregnancy. All new facial tingling should be immediately discussed with a health care provider.

What to Watch For:

If the tingling is on one side of the face, it might be related to nerve issues however cardiovascular issues, such as a stroke can also cause tingling to one side of the face.
