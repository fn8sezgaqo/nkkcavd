Finasteride is a medication often used to treat hair loss and prostate issues. Some research indicates that it may lead to persistent sexual side effects for some individuals. According to the studies by Irwig and Ganzer, there are reports of ongoing sexual, emotional, and cognitive problems even after discontinuing the drug. This condition is sometimes referred to as post-finasteride syndrome.

Key Points

Persistent Side Effects: Some people report sexual side effects like decreased libido and erectile dysfunction that continue after stopping finasteride.

Additional Concerns: Emotional and cognitive issues have also been noted in some cases.

Research Limitations: While these studies raise important concerns, more research is needed to fully understand how often these side effects occur and why they persist in some individuals.
