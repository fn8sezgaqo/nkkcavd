# Is greenish vaginal discharge during pregnancy normal? | Ubie Doctor's Note

Is greenish vaginal discharge during pregnancy normal?

Reviewed by Suo Lee, MD

Internal Medicine

[Doctor's Note](https://tendonforge.dns0663.com/blog/1079112935.html)

Greenish vaginal discharge in pregnancy is not usually normal and can be a sign of an infection. It’s important to have it checked by a doctor for proper evaluation and care.

Important Next Steps

Explanation

During pregnancy, it is normal to have a thin, milky vaginal discharge known as leukorrhea. This type of discharge is usually white or clear. However, if you notice that the discharge is greenish, it could be a sign that something is not right. Greenish discharge may indicate an infection, such as a bacterial infection or a different type of vaginitis not caused by yeast. Studies have shown that abnormal vaginal discharge in pregnancy can sometimes be linked to adverse health outcomes if infections are not treated early. This type of discharge can also come with other symptoms like an unpleasant smell, irritation, or discomfort. It is very important to pay attention to any changes in color, amount, or smell, especially during pregnancy when both the mother and baby are more sensitive to infections. If you notice a greenish discharge or any other unusual signs, make sure to reach out to a healthcare provider. They can evaluate your symptoms, do the necessary tests, and offer the right treatment to help keep you and your baby healthy.
