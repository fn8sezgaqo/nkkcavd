Explanation

When you feel pain on the right side of your body as a female, there are several things that might be causing it. Sometimes, the pain could be related to the reproductive organs. For example, an ectopic pregnancy—when a fertilized egg implants outside the uterus—can create sharp pain on one side. Ovarian cysts, which are fluid-filled sacs on the ovaries, may also cause pain if they grow or burst. Infections like pelvic inflammatory disease (PID) can lead to pain on one side of the lower abdomen because the reproductive organs become inflamed. On the other hand, pain on the right side might not always come from the reproductive system. Appendicitis, which is the inflammation of the appendix, generally causes pain in the lower right abdomen and is a serious condition. Sometimes, even a muscle or joint issue can play a part. Because the causes of right-side pain in females vary so much, it’s very important to see a doctor who can carefully check your history and do the needed tests. This will help determine the exact cause for your pain and guide the proper treatment to help you feel better.
