Explanation

Unexpected weight gain can be caused by several factors.

Medication Effects: Some medications, like insulin used in diabetes, can lead to weight gain. This happens because insulin helps the body store more sugar and fat.

Hormonal Changes: Hormones play a big role in weight. For example, after treating hyperthyroidism, some people gain weight because their metabolism slows down.

Other Causes: There are many other reasons for weight gain, like diet changes, lack of physical activity, or other health issues.

If you are worried about weight gain, you can start by using a free symptom checker to help identify possible causes, and then talk to a healthcare provider who can help find the reason and suggest ways to manage it.
