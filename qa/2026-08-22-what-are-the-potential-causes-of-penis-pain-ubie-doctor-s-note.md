Penis pain can result from different causes. One possible cause is chronic penile pain, which might stem from nerve issues or infections. Another cause is painful penile scar tissue related to Peyronie's disease, where scar tissue forms inside the penis, leading to pain and curvature.

Here are some potential causes of penis pain:

Infections: Infections can lead to pain and discomfort in the penis. These might be caused by bacteria or viruses.

Injuries: Physical injuries or trauma to the penis can result in pain.

Peyronie's Disease: This is a condition where scar tissue develops inside the penis, causing pain and bending, especially during erections.

Other Conditions: Sometimes, other less common diseases can cause penis pain.

If you experience penis pain, it is important to talk to a healthcare provider to determine the cause and get appropriate treatment.
