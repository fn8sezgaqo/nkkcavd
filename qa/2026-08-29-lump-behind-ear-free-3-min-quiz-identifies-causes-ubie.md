About the Symptom

A lump behind the ear can be due to swollen lymph nodes, cysts, infections, or less commonly, tumors. Most are benign, but persistent or growing lumps warrant evaluation.

When to See a Doctor

Seek professional care if you experience any of the following symptoms

Lump growing rapidly

Fixed or hard lump

Discharge or pus from lump

High fever

Lump persists longer than 2 weeks
