[What should do](https://satietybrief.china-akan.com/)

Splinter hemorrhages on the toenail look like small, dark lines under the nail. They can happen when you bump your toe or from other causes. Sometimes, they can be a sign of a health problem.

Possible Causes:

Injury: Most often, these happen because you hit or hurt your toe.

Health Issues: In some cases, they might be linked to things like infections or other health conditions like inflammation of your blood vessels.

[toenail Ubie](https://thymosin-report.hbsnzj.com/blog/0559620780.html)

What to Do:

Check for Injury: Think about if you hurt your toe recently.

[should do if notice](https://mindpeptide.bbsxyxy.com/blog/4550787661.html)

See a Doctor: If you didn’t hurt your toe or if you have more than one splinter hemorrhage, it’s a good idea to visit a doctor. They can help find out if there’s a health issue.

Remember: It’s always better to be safe and have a doctor check it out, especially if it doesn’t go away or if you find more.
