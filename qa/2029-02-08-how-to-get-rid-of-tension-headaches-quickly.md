How Long do Tension Headaches Last?

Tension headaches are often categorized into two main types: episodic tension-type headaches and chronic tension-type headaches. The main differentiation between these two types is how long the headaches last. Episodic tension-type headaches can last fewer than 15 days, while chronic tension-type headaches can last more than 15 days.

Episodic tension headaches can last 30 minutes to a few days, and it usually starts slowly with mild symptoms that can lead to stronger symptoms as the day goes by. Chronic headaches can last for a long time, and while the symptoms can waver throughout the days, the pain is always there.

How Does a Tension Headache Feel?

When you are suffering from a tension headache, you will almost feel like there is a tight band around your head. You will experience dull and aching pain around your head and pressure across your forehead or the sides or back of your head. People suffering from tension headaches often complain about tenderness in their scalp, shoulder muscles, and neck area.
