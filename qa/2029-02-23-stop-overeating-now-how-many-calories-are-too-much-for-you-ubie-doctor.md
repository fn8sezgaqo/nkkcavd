How can I determine how many calories are too much for my daily intake?

Internal Medicine

How many calories are too many depends on your diet type, food choices, and lifestyle. Ultra-processed foods are a major culprit, often causing people to unknowingly consume hundreds of extra calories per day. Portion sizes, added sugars, and low-nutrient snacks also contribute to calorie overload, which can lead to weight gain, fatigue, and long-term health risks.

When figuring out how many calories are too much, it's important to look at the types of food you eat. Research shows that ultra-processed foods can make you eat more calories than you need. These foods are often less filling, so you might eat more without realizing it.

Watch Ultra-Processed Foods: Eating a lot of these can lead to taking in more calories. Try to eat more whole foods like fruits, vegetables, and grains.

Fiber Matters: Foods high in fiber can help you feel full. This might help you eat the right amount of calories.

Be Careful with Cutting Calories: Simply eating fewer calories might not always help with weight loss. It's important to focus on the quality of the food, not just the number of calories.
