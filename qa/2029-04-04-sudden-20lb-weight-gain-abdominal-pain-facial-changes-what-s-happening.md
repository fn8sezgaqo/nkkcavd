Sudden 20lb Weight Gain, Abdominal Pain, Facial Changes: What's Happening?

20s, Female

[Happening Ubie](https://peplawllc.com/blog/3105169840.html)

Within the past 3 weeks, I have gained 20 pounds (which has never happened to me before, much less so quickly) and my stomach is suddenly larger than the rest of my body and painful. My hair suddenly looks and feels different, and my facial shape and features look noticeably different than they did 6 months ago. I have had the same diet and exercise habits for years, and have been on all my medications for at least 2 years. I’m very concerned about what is happening to me. Changing so quickly has made me not recognize myself.
