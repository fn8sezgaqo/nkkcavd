Can arthritis pain radiate down the leg similar to sciatica?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Arthritis pain from hip osteoarthritis can radiate down the leg, but it is not the same as sciatica. Hip arthritis pain typically originates in the groin, buttock, or outer hip and may travel to the thigh or knee, while sciatica stems from nerve compression in the lower spine and causes sharp, shooting pain down the back of the leg, often past the knee.

Because these conditions share overlapping symptoms but require very different treatments, identifying the true source of your pain is essential to getting relief.
