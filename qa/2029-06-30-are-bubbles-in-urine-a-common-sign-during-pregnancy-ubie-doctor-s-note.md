Explanation

Bubbles in urine can sometimes be seen during pregnancy. This might happen if there is protein in the urine, known as proteinuria.

Proteinuria: This is when there is too much protein in the urine. It can be a sign of a problem with the kidneys.

Pregnancy Considerations: According to the references, protein in urine can be important to monitor during pregnancy. It can sometimes mean that the kidneys are working harder than usual.

What to Do: If you see bubbles in your urine while pregnant, it is a good idea to talk to your doctor. They can check to make sure everything is okay.

Remember, it is always best to talk to a healthcare provider if you are worried about anything during your pregnancy.
