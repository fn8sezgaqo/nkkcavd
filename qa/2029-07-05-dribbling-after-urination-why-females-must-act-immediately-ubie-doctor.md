Explanation

Dribbling after urination in females can be caused by several conditions. One of the main reasons is urinary incontinence, which is when someone has trouble controlling their bladder. This can happen because the muscles that help hold urine in get weak. Another reason could be pelvic organ prolapse. This is when the bladder or other organs in the pelvis drop from their normal position and push against the vagina, making it hard to fully empty the bladder.

Sometimes, dribbling might also be linked to urethrovaginal reflux. This means that some urine flows back into the vagina during urination and then dribbles out afterward. This is more common in younger people but can happen to adults too.
