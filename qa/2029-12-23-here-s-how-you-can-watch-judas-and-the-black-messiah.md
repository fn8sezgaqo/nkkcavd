In the midst of Black History Month, Shaka King’s Judas and the Black Messiah is all the buzz, telling the true story of a Chicago chapter of the Black Panther Party during the late '60s.

The film stars Daniel Kaluuya as Fred Hampton, an Illinois Black Panther chairman, and depicts his life and the events that led to his 1969 assassination by the Chicago Police Department. Alongside Kaluuya is LaKeith Stanfield who plays William O’Neal, career thief turned FBI informant who played a significant role in Hampton’s death.
