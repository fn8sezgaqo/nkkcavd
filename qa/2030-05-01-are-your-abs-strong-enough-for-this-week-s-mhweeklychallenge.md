# Are Your Abs Strong Enough For This Week's #MHWeeklyChallenge?

Recent MH Weekly Challenges have tasked you with completing as many reps as possible of one or two movements (like, say, double-unders and mountain climbers) within a set amount of time. But this week you can take as long as you’d like!

That said, you’ll probably still want to get it over with as fast as possible.

[Abs Strong Enough](https://skincare.bairibu.com/compare/jumiso-snail-mucin-95-peptide-essence-versus-perricone-md-cold-plasma-plus-the-essence-6a5d39c68225/)

This week, your objective is simple: Complete 100 situps as quickly as you can. But, before you hit the deck, there’s just one rule.

At the top of each minute, you’re going to do three burpees, explains Carlos Davila, a trainer at New York City’s Fhitting Room.

[Enough For](https://science.peptidevirtue.com/article/round-lab-1025-dokdo-cleanser-versus-skintific-amino-acid-ultra-gentle-cleansing-mousse-e02733d90c38/)

Watch the video above for the full instructions and to watch Davila demonstrate how to do both the situp and burpee with proper form. Remember, when you sacrifice your form you’re only cheating yourself. And when you sacrifice form while racing the clock, you open the door to potential injuries.

So, let’s recap. You’re going to start the clock and go straight into three burpees. The second you complete that third burpee, it’s right into situps for the remainder of the minute, i.e. until the clock strikes 2:00. Then three more burpees, followed by your second round of situps. Repeat until you hit 100 sit-ups; the goal is to get the lowest time.

The faster you finish, the fewer burpees you’ll have to do, which should be motivation enough. But if it isn't, remember to post your time on social media so we (@menshealthmag) and Davila (@babalos) can see how you did; and be sure to include the hashtag #MHWeeklyChallenge.
